Formula used after you provide all three policy inputs: hourly base = eligible monthly pay ÷ monthly divisor; estimated OT = hourly base × multiplier × OT hours.

OT Assistant

Rule-based answers calculated from your loaded sheet records. No AI API required.

What you can ask

• Total OT hours or average OT per entry

• Highest OT and matching date / shift

• OT by month or by shift

• OT for a specific date, month, or year

• Records with zero OT or more than a given number of hours

• How many records or OT days are logged

If a date is ambiguous, include the year. The assistant does not guess payroll policy or official OT rates.
